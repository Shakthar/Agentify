# Pricing: Agentfy

Updated October 2026. Monthly billing, no lock-in. Euro is the base currency; BRL prices are the euro price converted and rounded up to the next value ending in 9. Credits are the same in EUR and BRL.

## Free
- Price: EUR 0 / R$0
- Credits: 1,000 (one time)
- Agents: 1 (1 WhatsApp number), website chat, knowledge base
- Models: Claude Haiku, GPT-4o Mini

## Starter
- Price: EUR 49 per month / R$279 per month
- Credits: 18,000 per month (about 450 conversations)
- Agents: 1; file uploads; add-ons available (Sales and Delivery, Payments, Booking)
- Models: adds Claude Sonnet; email support

## Business
- Price: EUR 129 per month / R$729 per month
- Credits: 49,000 per month (about 1,250 conversations)
- Agents: 3; Payments, Booking and Feedback included; mood detection; API access; 1-year history
- Models: adds GPT-4o

## Enterprise
- Price: EUR 349 per month / R$1,959 per month
- Credits: 134,000 per month (about 3,350 conversations)
- Agents: 10; all skills (except Sales and Delivery and custom skills); white-label included; Claude Opus; 24h SLA; unlimited history

## Extras
- Extra credits: pack of 5,000 credits: EUR 16 (Starter), EUR 15 (Business), EUR 14 (Enterprise)
- Meta's own WhatsApp message fees are billed by Meta to the number owner and are not included.

More detail: https://agentfy.shaklabs.tech/#precos
